## what I asked:
I asked for help creating an `instructions.html` page about the safe use of a gas cylinder using HTML only, without CSS or JavaScript.

## What I got:
I got a page containing a main heading, a paragraph explaining who the instructions are for, an unordered list of required equipment, an ordered list of safety steps, and a section explaining what to do if something goes wrong. I also learned how to use `<strong>`, `<em>`, and `<abbr>` correctly.

## What I changed and why:
I organized the information into clear sections to make the instructions easy to follow. I used an unordered list for the equipment because it does not need to be collected in a particular order, and an ordered list for the safety steps because they must be followed in sequence. I also added HTML comments above both lists to explain my choices and used `<strong>` to highlight important safety warnings.
