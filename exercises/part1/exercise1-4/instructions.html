Safe Use of a Gas Cylinder

These instructions are for anyone using a domestic cooking gas cylinder at home. Follow them when preparing to use the gas, checking the equipment, or finishing cooking.

What You Need

Prepare the necessary equipment before using your gas cylinder. Use only equipment that is suitable for your cylinder and appliance.

  • A compatible gas cylinder and cooking appliance
  • An approved regulator and suitable gas hose
  • A suitable leak-detection solution, such as soapy water
  • A well-ventilated cooking area

Steps

Follow these steps in order. If you suspect a leak or notice damaged equipment, do not proceed with using the gas.

  1. Place the cylinder upright in a stable, well-ventilated location, away from flames, heat, and ignition sources.
  2. Check the cylinder, regulator, and hose for visible damage. Never use damaged equipment or equipment that may leak.
  3. Ensure the cylinder valve is closed before connecting compatible equipment, and follow the manufacturer's instructions for making the connection.
  4. Check the connection for leaks using an appropriate leak-detection solution, following the manufacturer's instructions. Never use a flame to check for leaks.
  5. If there is no sign of a leak and the equipment is correctly connected, operate the appliance according to its instructions.
  6. When finished, turn off the appliance and close the cylinder valve if it is safe to do so.

If Something Goes Wrong

If you smell gas or suspect a leak, do not light a flame, smoke, or operate electrical switches. If safe, shut off the gas supply without putting yourself at risk. Leave the area, keep others away, and contact your gas supplier or local emergency service from a safe location. If the leak cannot be safely controlled, do not re-enter until professionals say it is safe.
