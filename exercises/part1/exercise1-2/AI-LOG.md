## What I asked:
I asked for help creating a `how-to.html` page explaining how to open a bank account in The Gambia using HTML.

## What I got:
I got a webpage with a main heading, sections explaining what to do before visiting the bank, the documents to bring, what to do at the bank, and what happens after applying. I also included subsections for identification documents, proof of address, completing the application, and reviewing account terms.

## What I changed and why:
I added examples of banks available in The Gambia, such as Ecobank and GTBank, to make the information more relevant to local readers. I also organized the information into headings and paragraphs so that readers can follow the process easily and understand what they need to prepare before visiting a bank.
