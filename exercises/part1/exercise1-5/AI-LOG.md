## What I asked:
I wanted to create an About Me page for my personal website using HTML and include information about my background, interests, and career goals.

## What I got:
I created an About Me page with a heading, navigation links, personal introduction, interests, educational background, and a footer. I also used semantic HTML elements such as `<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, and `<footer>`. I included `<strong>` and `<em>` to understand how to mark important information and emphasize words.

## What I changed and why:
I added my educational background at Civil Service University, my CompTIA A+ training, and my interest in web development and cybersecurity. I also added an “If something goes wrong” section to demonstrate how to use important text and emphasis correctly in HTML. These changes made the page more personal and helped me understand how to structure a webpage properly.
