TEC SQUAD Car Wash

TEC SQUAD Car Wash provides vehicle cleaning services to help keep cars clean and presentable. We serve customers in The Gambia. Our services include exterior washing, interior cleaning, and additional vehicle cleaning options.

About Our Car Wash

We aim to provide convenient and affordable car washing services for vehicle owners. Whether you need a quick wash or a more thorough cleaning, you can choose a service that suits your needs.

Our Car Wash Services

We offer different cleaning services depending on the condition of your vehicle and the type of cleaning you require.

Exterior Washing

Exterior washing removes dirt and dust from the outside of your vehicle, helping it maintain a clean appearance.

  • Small cars: D150
  • Sedans: D200
  • SUVs and 4x4 vehicles: D250

Interior Cleaning

Interior cleaning helps remove dust and dirt from the inside of your vehicle. Prices depend on the vehicle size and the level of cleaning required.

How to Get Your Car Washed
